# Thought Lens v0.1

A Thought Lens is an interpretive mapping applied to an Axis Thought Packet.

It answers:

```text
How should this thought state be seen from this viewpoint?
```

## Lens Components

- `viewpoint` - the human, AI agent, community, or system reading the packet
- `purpose` - why the packet is being interpreted
- `selection` - which parts of the packet are relevant
- `translation` - how the packet should be reframed
- `output` - the form produced by the lens

## Example Lenses

### Publication Lens

Transforms a private working thought into a public research artifact.

### Agent Handoff Lens

Transforms an active task state into instructions and context for another AI agent.

### Review Lens

Transforms a packet into risks, missing evidence, and unresolved assumptions.

## Relationship to Thought Packets

A Thought Packet preserves state.

A Thought Lens interprets state.

Together, they support continuity and controlled transformation across collaborative systems.
